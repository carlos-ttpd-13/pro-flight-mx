Our Aviation Services

We offer comprehensive aviation administrative support for pilots, flight attendants, aircraft maintenance technicians, and other aviation professionals in Mexico.

1. Aviation Licenses

  • Issuance of fixed-wing Commercial Pilot (PAC) licenses.
  • Renewal of Commercial Pilot (PAC) licenses.
  • Renewal of Airline Transport Pilot (TPI) licenses.
  • Reinstatement of Commercial Pilot (PAC) licenses.
  • Reinstatement of Airline Transport Pilot (TPI) licenses.
  • Issuance of flight attendant licenses.
  • Renewal of flight attendant licenses.
  • Reinstatement of flight attendant licenses.
  • Renewal of aircraft maintenance technician licenses.
  • Administrative assistance with Class 2 aircraft maintenance technician licenses in Avionics, Engines, and Airframes.
  • Administrative and legal follow-up for aviation license reinstatement.

2. Aircraft Ratings and Endorsements

  • Assistance with Hawker, Cessna, Learjet, and Pilatus aircraft ratings.
  • Assistance with Airbus A320 type ratings.
  • Assistance with Learjet 30/35, 45, and 55 series ratings.
  • Assistance with initial RTARI authorization.
  • Renewal of RTARI authorization.
  • Assistance with adding RTARI authorization to a license.
  • Assistance with single-engine ratings.
  • Assistance with multi-engine ratings.
  • Assistance with instrument ratings.

3. Aviation Medicine

  • Scheduling assistance for aviation medical examinations.
  • Account setup for aviation medical appointments.
  • Guidance on aeromedical fitness evaluations for aviation personnel.
  • Assistance with urgent medical appointments, subject to availability.
  • Follow-up on appointments with Mexico’s Preventive Medicine for Transportation authority.
  • Scheduling assistance with authorized independent medical examiners.
  • Guidance on documents and requirements for aviation medical examinations.
Important note: ProFlight MX does not guarantee medical fitness results. Only an authorized medical examiner can determine whether an applicant is FIT or UNFIT.

4. Aviation Courses and Training

  • CRM – Crew Resource Management.
  • CFIT – Controlled Flight Into Terrain.
  • ALAR – Approach and Landing Accident Reduction.
  • SMS – Safety Management System.
  • AVSEC – Aviation Security.
  • MERPEL – Dangerous Goods.
  • Recurrent training for pilots and flight attendants.
  • Assistance with aeromedical courses for pilots and flight attendants.
  • Assistance with urgently needed aviation courses, subject to availability.

5. Aviation Certificates and Documentation

  • Assistance obtaining accident- and incident-free certificates.
  • Guidance on certifying flight hours in pilot logbooks.
  • Review of aviation records and files.
  • Compilation of documents for license issuance.
  • Compilation of documents for license renewal.
  • Guidance on documentation requirements of Mexico’s Federal Civil Aviation Agency (AFAC).

6. Administrative Assistance and AFAC Follow-up

  • Follow-up on aviation license applications.
  • Follow-up on aircraft rating and endorsement applications.
  • Assistance with application and tracking reference numbers.
  • Assistance requesting changes to license collection locations.
  • Administrative representation using a letter of authorization, when permitted.
  • Follow-up on files and pending applications.
  • Guidance on official government fee payments.
  • Guidance on aviation administrative requirements and procedures.
  • Personalized assistance bringing aviation personnel documentation into compliance.
  • Administrative follow-up with the aviation authority.
